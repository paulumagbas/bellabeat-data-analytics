## Portfolio / LinkedIn project entry

**Bellabeat Leaf: Activity Patterns and Recording Limitations**

Analyzed historical Fitbit activity and sleep data using Google BigQuery SQL and
Tableau to develop marketing hypotheses for a Bellabeat Leaf case study. Preserved
raw data, removed three exact extra sleep records, and validated participant-date
joins across 940 activity records and 410 cleaned sleep records. Distinguished
participant-equal averages from daily-row means and examined how recording
coverage affected sleep summaries and activity-sleep correlations. Built a
four-chart dashboard covering participant activity, weekday patterns, sleep
coverage and exploratory associations. Proposed customer research and controlled
messaging tests while documenting sample limitations and avoiding causal or
business-uplift claims.

**Tools:** Google BigQuery (GoogleSQL), Tableau Desktop/Public, CSV and Markdown.
